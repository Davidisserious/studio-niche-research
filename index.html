A private research tool for planning original videos

Studio Niche Research is a small tool built and used by one independent creator, David-Emmanuel Owoicho, who makes original, faceless educational videos (animated history, science and explainer videos) for their own YouTube channels.

The tool reads public video and channel statistics through the YouTube Data API to see which topics and video formats audiences are watching now, so the creator can plan new, original videos people actually want.

What it does

  • Searches public long-form videos published in the last 30 days, by topic.
  • Reads public statistics for those videos and their channels (view counts, upload dates, durations).
  • Ranks channels for the creator's own research, on the creator's own computer.

What it never does

  • It never signs anyone in, never asks for a Google account, and never reads private data.
  • It never uploads, comments, likes, subscribes or changes anything on YouTube.
  • It never downloads or stores videos.
  • Its results are never published, sold or shared. They are seen only by the creator.
  • It deletes the YouTube data it keeps within 30 days.
