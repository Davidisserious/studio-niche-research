Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

1. YouTube API Services

Studio Niche Research ("the tool") uses YouTube API Services. By using the tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Who uses the tool

The tool is private. It is used only by its owner, David-Emmanuel Owoicho, on their own computer, for research for their own YouTube channels. It has no public user accounts and no sign-in.

3. What information the tool accesses

The tool accesses only public YouTube data through the YouTube Data API: public video information (titles, publish dates, durations, view counts, thumbnails, the "made for kids" setting) and public channel information (channel names, creation dates, subscriber and view counts, public upload lists).

The tool does not access any private data, does not use Google sign-in (OAuth), and does not collect personal information about the people whose public videos it reads. It collects no information about visitors to this website.

4. How the information is used

The public data is used only to rank topics, video formats and channels for the owner's own planning of original videos. Thumbnails are shown unmodified and checked by an automated image check in memory only; they are never saved.

5. How the information is stored and deleted

  • Data is stored only on the owner's own computer, never on a server.
  • Responses are cached for under 24 hours to avoid repeat requests.
  • All stored YouTube data (reports, results and caches) is deleted automatically within 30 days, by a daily clean-up task.
  • The owner's own notes (for example "this channel is faceless") are kept by channel ID only, with no YouTube titles or other API data.

6. Sharing

The information is not shared with anyone, not sold, and not published. No third parties serve content or advertisements through the tool.

7. Cookies and device information

The tool runs locally and does not place cookies or collect device information. This website sets no cookies of its own.

8. Your choices

The tool never receives access to anyone's Google account, so there is no access to revoke. If you are a YouTube creator and want any data about your public channel removed from the owner's computer before the automatic 30-day deletion, contact the owner below and it will be deleted within 7 days. You can also manage apps connected to your Google account at Google security settings (this tool will never appear there).
